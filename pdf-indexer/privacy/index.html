PDF Indexer

Privacy Policy

Effective August 16, 2026

Local documents

Imported PDFs, title mappings, and search data remain in the app’s private storage. PDF Indexer does not upload them to publisher-operated servers. A mapping leaves only when you choose an export destination in the system share sheet. A cloud-backed file provider’s own terms still apply to its original file.

Advertising

PDF Indexer may request non-personalized banner ads from Google Mobile Ads after any privacy choices required in your region. Google may process IP-derived approximate location, device and app identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostic or performance data to deliver, measure, and protect ads. PDF Indexer does not send PDFs, mappings, titles, search queries, or document metadata to the ad service.

Control and deletion

Where required, ad privacy choices can be revisited from Privacy & Support. Deleting a document removes its index and app-managed PDF. Android cloud backup is disabled. On iOS, app data may be included in device or iCloud backups according to system settings.

Contact

For privacy requests, see PDF Indexer Support.
