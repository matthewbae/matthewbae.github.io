Jazz Lineage

Privacy Policy

Effective August 16, 2026

Catalog and searches

Jazz Lineage ships its catalog in the app and searches it on the device. It has no account system and does not send search terms, viewed artists, recordings, works, or albums to publisher-operated analytics services. When you choose an external music link, the destination service applies its own privacy practices.

Advertising

Jazz Lineage may request non-personalized banner ads from Google Mobile Ads after any privacy choices required in your region. Google may process IP-derived approximate location, device and app identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostic or performance data to deliver, measure, and protect ads. Jazz Lineage does not send searches or catalog browsing history to the ad service.

Control

Where required, ad privacy choices can be revisited from About. Removing the app asks the operating system to remove its local data, subject to device backup behavior.

Contact

For privacy requests, see Jazz Lineage Support.
