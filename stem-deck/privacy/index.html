Stem Deck

Privacy Policy

Effective August 16, 2026

Local audio processing

Imported audio, generated stems, waveform summaries, mixer settings, loop points, and project metadata stay in the app’s private storage. Separation runs on the device. Files leave only when you choose a destination in the system share sheet.

Advertising

Stem Deck may request non-personalized banner ads from Google Mobile Ads after any privacy choices required in your region. Google may process IP-derived approximate location, device and app identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostic or performance data to deliver, measure, and protect ads. Stem Deck does not send audio, stems, project names, waveforms, or mixer settings to the ad service.

Control and deletion

Where required, ad privacy choices can be revisited from About. Delete a session to remove its imported source, stems, manifest, and settings. Optional model files can be removed separately. Uninstalling asks the operating system to remove private app storage, subject to device backup behavior.

Contact

For privacy requests, see Stem Deck Support.
